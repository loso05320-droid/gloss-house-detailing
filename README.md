# Gloss House Detailing

I'll build Gloss House a full brand and one-page site: dark luxury look, services, booking, and reviews. First I'm loading the design guidance.

The Gloss House site is published as a private one-page page. It has a dark plum-and-champagne look with a car whose paint catches a moving light, then services, the review, a four-step process and a booking form. It's private until you share it from the page's Share menu.

I couldn't preview it here, so I haven't seen how it looks on a phone or in light mode. Open it on your phone and tell me anything that looks off.

Reviews: I used only what you pasted. Bret Karlovich's review is the featured quote, with the "punctuality" and "professionalism" tags beside it. Michael Freeman's review came through with no text or rating, so I left him out. I also tidied "attn" to "attention" and put Gloss House in capitals. Paste more reviews and I'll build out the section.

Claims I couldn't confirm: The bullet lists under each service are my own wording. "5+ years with local families" comes from the first review saying "for years" five years ago. Please check both.

Contact details: I didn't have a phone, email or address, so none appear yet. They're blank at the top of the page's script and will show up once filled in.

Booking form: It can't send anything on its own. It builds a formatted request the customer copies and sends to Gloss House, and it never claims the request was sent. If you give me an email or phone number, I'll show it next to the form.

To go further, I'd need real photos, prices, the service area, and hours or the booking process. With those I can add a before-and-after gallery, price tiers and a map.
